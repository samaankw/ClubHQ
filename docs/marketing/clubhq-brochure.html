Our Story

Built to solve one club's real problem first.

ClubHQ started as a private tool for one business: Williams Soccer Clinic, a youth soccer training business founded by twin brothers Samaan and Samir Williams, running sessions across three Atlanta-area locations: Dunwoody, Snellville, and Stone Mountain.

Spreadsheets, group texts, and generic scheduling apps could handle a calendar. None of them understood what a coach actually does day to day: evaluate a player, track their development, assign real homework, and follow up on it weeks later. So the brothers built the system they wished existed, for their own business first.

We didn't research a market and guess what clubs might want. We ran a club on it ourselves, every week, until it actually worked.

Once it was carrying the full weight of a real, paying training business, three locations, real families, real coaches, it stopped being an internal tool and became a platform other clubs and private trainers could run on too.

Why ClubHQ

Everything a club actually needs, in one place.

Most club software handles a calendar and stops there. ClubHQ was built around the full coaching workflow, the parts that actually determine whether players improve and parents stay engaged.

Coach-led development

03

Payments, without a processor

Lightweight paid or unpaid tracking per player per month, visible to staff and to the parent actually being asked to pay.

04

Built for real families

Player Development

AI, Handled Responsibly

A player's real name never reaches the AI

AI helps write the plan. It never learns who your players are.

ClubHQ uses AI in three places: turning a coach's evaluation into a draft development plan, powering a director's data assistant, and transcribing a coach's spoken voice notes into structured player updates. In every one of those, the actual child's name is stripped out before anything is sent, and only put back afterward, on ClubHQ's own server, never inside the request to the AI itself.
